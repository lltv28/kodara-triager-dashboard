Definitions & data notes
  • Source: PostHog project 572719, refreshed daily. Months and last 7/30/90 days use complete America/Los_Angeles days through yesterday. Weeks start Monday and are clipped to the selected range. Coverage begins September 1, 2026; earlier dates are unavailable. New months appear after their first complete day.
  • Unique people: Each person is counted once per event or step within the selected period. Whole-period totals are independently deduplicated and may not equal the sum of the weekly rows.
  • Widget loaded: Successfully initialized widgets with captured analytics, not all website visitors or load attempts. This report covers production tracking on kodarahealth.com and go.kodara.com; www and preview origins are not included by the checked analytics configuration.
  • Q1: Completed the video-readiness question. Email, business, revenue and time selected count completed steps. Calendar counts people shown the day-selection step.
  • Booked: People with a tracked booking completion, not uncancelled appointments or attended calls.
  • Percentages: Ratios of activity counts within the selected period, not ordered same-person conversion rates. Qualification intentionally filters some people out, so differences between steps are not all abandonment.
  • Exclusions: Known internal users, Kodara team addresses, example.com tests and identified personal test accounts. Unidentified tests or bots may remain.
